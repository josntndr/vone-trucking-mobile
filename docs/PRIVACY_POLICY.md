# Vone Trucking Privacy Policy

**Effective Date**: August 22, 2024  
**Last Updated**: August 22, 2024

## Introduction

Welcome to Vone Trucking ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

By using the Vone Trucking app, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use the App.

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Personal Information

We collect the following personal information that you provide directly:

- **Account Information**: Name, email address, phone number, role (operator/driver/porter)
- **Employment Information**: Employee ID, license number (drivers), identification numbers
- **Profile Information**: Profile photo (optional), emergency contact information

### 1.2 Location Data

**Real-Time Location Tracking**:
- We collect precise GPS location data when you use trip tracking features
- Location is collected continuously during active trips
- Location data may be collected in the background when the app is not in active use
- Location history is stored for trip route reconstruction and analytics

**Purpose of Location Collection**:
- Track trip progress and vehicle location
- Verify pickup and delivery locations
- Calculate trip distances and routes
- Monitor driver safety and adherence to routes
- Generate location-based analytics and reports

**User Control**:
- You can disable location services in device settings
- Disabling location will prevent you from starting or completing trips
- Location data can be deleted upon request

### 1.3 Trip and Business Data

- **Trip Information**: Pickup/dropoff locations, scheduled times, cargo details, trip income
- **Fuel Records**: Fuel litres, costs, fuel station locations, receipt photos
- **Expense Records**: Toll amounts, parking fees, maintenance costs, receipt images
- **Proof of Delivery**: Recipient signatures, delivery photos, delivery times, customer names
- **Vehicle Information**: Truck registration, make, model, odometer readings
- **Financial Data**: Payroll information, cash advances, trip profitability calculations

### 1.4 Photos and Media

- **Receipt Photos**: Images of fuel and expense receipts
- **Delivery Photos**: Images of delivered cargo
- **Signature Images**: Digital signatures from delivery recipients
- **Incident Photos**: Images documenting accidents or incidents (if applicable)

**Storage and Processing**:
- Photos are stored locally on your device until uploaded
- Photos are encrypted during transmission
- Photos are stored securely on our servers
- You can delete photos associated with your account

### 1.5 Device and Usage Information

- **Device Information**: Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers
- **Usage Data**: App features used, screen views, button clicks, session duration
- **Performance Data**: Crash reports, error logs, app performance metrics
- **Network Information**: Network type (WiFi, cellular), connection status

### 1.6 Notifications

- **Push Notification Tokens**: Device tokens for sending notifications
- **Notification Preferences**: Your notification settings and quiet hours

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

### 2.1 Core App Functionality
- Provide trip management and tracking services
- Enable communication between operators, drivers, and helpers
- Record and track fuel consumption and expenses
- Capture and store proof of delivery
- Calculate trip profitability and generate reports

- Analyze fleet performance and utilization
- Identify trends in fuel consumption and expenses
- Monitor on-time delivery rates
- Generate business intelligence reports
- Improve app features and user experience
- Identify and fix technical issues

### 2.3 Notifications and Communication
- Send trip assignment notifications
- Alert about trip updates and changes
- Remind about upcoming schedules
- Notify about GPS disconnections
- Alert about document expirations and maintenance
- Send payroll and cash advance notifications

### 2.4 Security and Fraud Prevention
- Verify user identity and prevent unauthorized access
- Detect and prevent duplicate submissions
- Monitor for suspicious activity
- Protect against fraud and abuse

### 2.5 Legal Compliance
- Comply with legal obligations
- Enforce our Terms of Service
- Respond to legal requests and prevent harm

## 3. How We Share Your Information

### 3.1 Within Your Organization

Information is shared with other users in your organization based on role permissions:

- **Operators**: Can view all trip data, driver locations, and reports
- **Drivers**: Can view their assigned trips and personal payroll
- **Porters**: Can view trips where they are assigned and personal payroll

### 3.2 Service Providers

We may share information with trusted third-party service providers who assist us in:

- Cloud hosting and data storage (e.g., AWS, Google Cloud)
- Push notification delivery (Expo Push Notification Service)
- Analytics and performance monitoring
- Customer support services

**All service providers**:
- Are bound by confidentiality agreements
- Only access information necessary for their services
- Are prohibited from using your data for their own purposes

### 3.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required to do so by law or in response to:

- Valid legal process (court orders, subpoenas)
- Government or regulatory requests
- Protection of our rights, property, or safety
- Prevention of fraud or illegal activities

### 3.4 Business Transfers

In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred to the acquiring entity. You will be notified of any such change.

### 3.5 With Your Consent

We may share your information for purposes not described in this policy with your explicit consent.

## 4. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as necessary to provide our services and comply with legal obligations:

- **Active Account Data**: Retained while your account is active
- **Trip Data**: Retained for 7 years for accounting and tax purposes
- **Location History**: Retained for 2 years or as required by law
- **Photos and Receipts**: Retained for 3 years for audit purposes
- **Deleted Account Data**: Permanently deleted within 90 days of account closure, except where legal retention is required

You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us at privacy@vonetrucking.com.

## 5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information:

### 5.1 Technical Safeguards
- **Encryption**: All data is encrypted in transit using TLS/HTTPS
- **Secure Storage**: Data at rest is encrypted using AES-256
- **Access Controls**: Role-based access with strict permissions
- **Authentication**: Secure login with password requirements
- **Secure APIs**: All API endpoints require authentication

### 5.2 Operational Safeguards
- Regular security audits and penetration testing
- Employee training on data protection
- Incident response procedures
- Regular backups and disaster recovery plans

### 5.3 Device Security
- Offline data stored securely on device
- Automatic logout after inactivity
- Secure sync queue for offline operations
- Local data encryption

**However**, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 8. Location Services Disclosure

### 8.1 Background Location Access

**Why We Need It**:
The Vone Trucking app requires background location access to:
- Track driver location during trips even when app is in background
- Record route history for completed trips
- Provide real-time location updates to operators
- Calculate accurate trip distances

**How We Use It**:
- Location is only collected during active trips
- Location updates occur every 1-5 minutes during trips
- Location data is stored securely and used only for trip tracking
- Operators can view driver locations on a map in real-time

**User Control**:
- You must explicitly grant background location permission
- You can revoke location access at any time in device settings
- Revoking access will prevent trip tracking functionality
- Location permissions can be set to "While Using App" (limited functionality) or "Always" (full functionality)

### 8.2 Location Data Accuracy

Location data accuracy depends on:
- GPS signal strength
- Device capabilities
- Environmental factors (buildings, tunnels)
- Network connectivity

We cannot guarantee 100% accurate location data.

### 8.3 Location Data Sharing

Your location data is shared with:
- Operators in your organization (for trip monitoring)
- Service providers (for mapping and analytics)
- No third parties for marketing or other purposes
