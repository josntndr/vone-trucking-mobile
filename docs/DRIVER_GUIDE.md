## Getting Started

### First Time Setup

#### 1. Install the App
- **Android**: Download from Google Play Store
- **iOS**: Download from Apple App Store
- Search for "Vone Trucking"
- Tap "Install" / "Get"

#### 2. Login
Your operator will provide your login credentials:
- Open the app
- Enter your **email** and **password**
- Tap "Login"

#### 3. Grant Permissions

The app needs certain permissions to work:

**Location** (Required ✅):
- Why: To track your trip route
- When prompted, tap "Allow all the time"
- This is safe - location is only tracked during active trips

**Camera** (Optional):
- Why: To take photos of receipts and deliveries
- Tap "Allow" when prompted

**Notifications** (Recommended):
- Why: To receive trip assignments and updates
- Tap "Allow"

#### 4. Complete Your Profile
- Tap your name in top right
- Add a profile photo (optional)
- Verify your phone number
- Check your license information is correct

**Actions**:
- 🗺️ **Get Directions**: Open in Google Maps/Waze
- 📞 **Call Customer**: Contact recipient
- ▶️ **Start Trip**: Begin the trip (when ready)

## Starting a Trip

### Before You Start

✅ **Checklist**:
- [ ] Truck fueled and ready
- [ ] Cargo loaded
- [ ] Documents on board
- [ ] Phone charged
- [ ] Location services enabled
- [ ] Porter ready (if assigned)

### How to Start

1. Open the trip from your assignments
2. Tap the **green "Start Trip"** button
3. Enter starting odometer reading
   - Look at truck's odometer
   - Enter exact number (e.g., 45180)
4. Complete pre-trip checklist (if shown):
   - Check tire pressure
   - Check oil level
   - Check lights working
   - Cargo secured
5. Tap "Confirm Start"

**What Happens**:
- ✅ Your location is recorded
- ✅ Trip status changes to "In Progress"
- ✅ Operator is notified
- ✅ Location tracking begins
- ✅ Timer starts

### After Starting

- Trip moves to "Active" tab
- You'll see:
  - Time elapsed
  - Route on map
  - Your current location
  - Buttons to record fuel/expenses
  - Button to complete delivery

## Recording Fuel

### When to Record Fuel

Record fuel every time you refuel during the trip.

### How to Record Fuel

1. After fueling, open your active trip
2. Tap **"Add Fuel"** button
3. Enter fuel details:
   - **Fuel Station**: Name (e.g., "Shell Mlolongo")
   - **Litres**: Amount pumped (e.g., 45)
   - **Cost**: Total paid (e.g., 6750)
4. **Take Receipt Photo**:
   - Tap camera icon
   - Take clear photo of receipt
   - Ensure price and litres visible
5. Tap **"Save"**

**Tips**:
- ✅ Take receipt photo immediately
- ✅ Ensure receipt is readable
- ✅ Record fuel before leaving station
- ✅ Double-check litre and cost amounts
- ❌ Don't estimate - use exact amounts

### Offline Fuel Recording

If you have no internet:
- Record fuel as normal
- Photo and data saved locally
- Uploads automatically when online
- You'll see "Pending Sync" indicator

## Recording Expenses

### Types of Expenses

You can record:
- **Tolls**: Road toll fees
- **Parking**: Parking fees
- **Maintenance**: Emergency repairs
- **Other**: Any other trip expense

### How to Record Expenses

1. Open your active trip
2. Tap **"Add Expense"** button
3. Select expense type (Toll, Parking, etc.)
4. Enter amount (e.g., 300)
5. Add description (e.g., "Thika Superhighway toll")
6. Take receipt photo (if available):
   - Some tolls don't give receipts
   - Photo optional but recommended
7. Tap **"Save"**

### Expense Guidelines

**What to Record**:
- ✅ All toll fees
- ✅ Parking fees at delivery
- ✅ Emergency repairs during trip
- ✅ Any expense related to this trip

**What NOT to Record**:
- ❌ Personal expenses (food, drinks)
- ❌ Expenses not related to this trip
- ❌ Fuel (record in Fuel section)

**Receipt Photos**:
- Required for expenses over KES 500
- Helps verify expenses
- Ensures you get reimbursed

## Completing Delivery

### Proof of Delivery (POD)

When you arrive at delivery location:

1. Tap **"Complete Delivery"** button
2. Enter **Recipient Name**:
   - Person receiving cargo
   - First and last name
3. Capture **Signature**:
   - Hand phone to recipient
   - They sign with finger on screen
   - Tap "Done" when finished
4. Take **Delivery Photo**:
   - Photo of delivered cargo
   - Shows cargo in good condition
   - Can take multiple photos
5. Add **Delivery Notes** (optional):
   - Any special conditions
   - Recipient comments
   - Issues encountered
6. Verify **Delivery Time** is correct
7. Tap **"Save Proof of Delivery"**

### POD Tips

**Signature**:
- ✅ Get signature from authorized person
- ✅ Signature should be clear
- ✅ If recipient refuses, note reason and call operator
- ❌ Don't sign yourself
- ❌ Don't leave without signature

**Photos**:
- ✅ Clear, well-lit photos
- ✅ Show cargo condition
- ✅ Take from multiple angles
- ✅ Include any damage (report immediately)

**Notes**:
- Document any delivery issues
- Note if partial delivery
- Record recipient comments
- Report damage or shortage immediately

### Offline Delivery

If you have no internet:
- Complete POD as normal
- Signature and photos saved locally
- Uploads when back online
- Operator notified when synced

## Finishing the Trip

### When to Finish

Finish the trip after:
- ✅ Cargo delivered
- ✅ POD completed
- ✅ You've left delivery location
- ✅ All expenses recorded

### How to Finish Trip

1. Open your active trip
2. Tap **"Complete Trip"** button
3. Enter **ending odometer** reading:
   - Look at truck's odometer
   - Enter exact number (e.g., 45660)
4. Complete post-trip checklist (if shown):
   - Truck damage check
   - Cargo area clean
   - Documents returned
5. Review **Trip Summary**:
   - Distance traveled
   - Time taken
   - Fuel cost
   - Total expenses
   - Income
   - Profit
6. Tap **"Confirm Completion"**

**What Happens**:
- ✅ Trip marked complete
- ✅ Operator notified
- ✅ Location tracking stops
- ✅ Trip moved to "Completed" tab
- ✅ Trip added to your payroll

### Trip Summary

After completing, you can view:
- Total distance (calculated from odometer)
- Total time (from start to finish)
- Fuel used and cost
- All expenses
- Trip income
- Your earnings (if applicable)

## Offline Mode

### Working Without Internet

The app works offline! You can:
- ✅ View trip details
- ✅ Start trips
- ✅ Record fuel
- ✅ Record expenses
- ✅ Take photos
- ✅ Complete deliveries
- ✅ Finish trips

**Everything saved locally** and syncs when you're back online.

### Understanding Offline Mode

**Offline Indicator**:
- Red cloud icon = Offline
- Blue sync icon = Syncing
- Green checkmark = Synced

**Pending Items**:
- Tap sync icon to see pending items
- Shows what's waiting to upload
- Includes photos, fuel records, POD, etc.

**Auto-Sync**:
- Syncs automatically when online
- Syncs when you open app
- Syncs every 15 minutes
- Syncs when switching from WiFi to data

### Offline Tips

**Before Going Offline**:
- ✅ View trip details while online
- ✅ Download any maps you need
- ✅ Ensure app is up to date
- ✅ Check your assignments loaded

**While Offline**:
- ✅ Record everything as normal
- ✅ Take photos (saved locally)
- ✅ Don't worry about sync
- ✅ App handles everything

**When Back Online**:
- ✅ Open app to trigger sync
- ✅ Wait for all items to sync
- ✅ Check for green checkmark
- ✅ Verify POD uploaded

**Important**:
- Don't uninstall app with pending items
- Don't clear app data before syncing
- Keep phone charged for syncing

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

#### Can't Start Trip

**Problem**: Start button disabled or shows error

**Solutions**:
- Check if trip is scheduled for today
- Verify you don't have another active trip
- Ensure location services enabled
- Restart app and try again
- Contact operator if trip not showing

#### Location Not Working

**Problem**: "Location unavailable" message

**Solutions**:
1. Check device settings:
   - Settings > Apps > Vone Trucking > Permissions
   - Location must be "Allow all the time"
2. Enable GPS:
   - Settings > Location > Turn on
3. Restart device
4. Contact operator if persists

#### Photos Won't Upload

**Problem**: Receipt/delivery photos stuck

**Solutions**:
- Check internet connection
- Ensure sufficient storage space on phone
- Open app to trigger sync
- Navigate to Settings > Sync > Manual Sync
- Photos will upload automatically - be patient
- Contact support if stuck for 24+ hours

#### Can't Complete Delivery

**Problem**: POD save fails or button disabled

**Solutions**:
- Ensure signature captured
- Ensure at least one photo taken
- Check recipient name entered
- Try offline - saves locally
- Contact operator if urgent

#### App Crashes

**Problem**: App closes unexpectedly

**Solutions**:
1. Update app:
   - Check Play Store/App Store for update
2. Restart phone
3. Clear cache:
   - Settings > Apps > Vone Trucking > Storage > Clear Cache
   - Don't clear data!
4. Reinstall app (last resort):
   - Your login credentials saved
   - Pending syncs preserved
5. Contact support

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: When is location tracked?**  
A: Only during active trips. When you're off duty or between trips, location is not tracked.

**Q: What if I forget to record fuel?**  
A: Record it as soon as you remember. Include the station and receipt photo. If you don't have the receipt, note that in the description.

**Q: Can I edit a completed trip?**  
A: No, once completed, only your operator can edit. Contact them if you need changes.

**Q: What if customer refuses to sign?**  
A: Note the reason in delivery notes and contact your operator immediately. Take photos of delivered cargo as proof.

**Q: How do I know my pay?**  
A: Navigate to Payroll section to see your current period earnings. Pay is calculated based on completed trips.

**Q: What if I have a personal expense during trip?**  
A: Don't record personal expenses (food, drinks) in the app. Only record trip-related expenses that should be reimbursed.

**Q: Can I use the app on multiple phones?**  
A: Yes, log in with your credentials. However, only one device should have an active trip at a time.

**Q: What if I lose internet during a trip?**  
A: Continue as normal. Everything saves locally and syncs when you're back online.

**Q: How long are trips kept?**  
A: Your trip history is kept for 2 years. You can always view past trips in the Completed tab.

**Q: Can I delete a trip?**  
A: No, only operators can delete trips. Contact your operator if needed.
