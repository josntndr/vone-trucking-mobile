# Vone Trucking - Porter/Helper Guide

## Welcome Porter!

This simple guide will help you use the Vone Trucking app as a porter/helper. Your main job is to assist drivers with loading, unloading, and deliveries.

**Version**: 1.0  
**Last Updated**: August 22, 2024

---

## What You Need to Know

As a porter, you have **limited access** to the app. You can:
- ✅ View trips where you're assigned
- ✅ Help with delivery proof
- ✅ Record certain expenses (with permission)
- ✅ View your payroll

You **cannot**:
- ❌ Start or complete trips (driver does this)
- ❌ Record fuel (driver does this)
- ❌ View other trips
- ❌ Change trip details

---

## Getting Started

### 1. Install & Login

**Install App**:
- Download "Vone Trucking" from Play Store or App Store
- Tap "Install"

**Login**:
- Your operator will give you:
  - Email address
  - Password
- Open app and enter credentials
- Tap "Login"

### 2. Grant Permissions

**Camera** (Optional):
- For taking delivery photos
- Tap "Allow" if you'll help with photos

**Notifications** (Recommended):
- To know when you're assigned to trips
- Tap "Allow"

## Your Assignments

### Viewing Your Trips

When you open the app:
- See trips where you're assigned as porter
- Trips organized by:
  - **Upcoming**: Future trips
  - **Active**: Current trip
  - **Completed**: Past trips

### Trip Information

Tap any trip to see:
- 📅 Date and time
- 🚛 Truck
- 👤 Driver you're working with
- 📍 Pickup and dropoff locations
- 📦 Cargo details
- ⚖️ Weight

You can also:
- See route on map
- Call driver
- Call customer (if needed)

---

## During the Trip

### Your Role

As porter, you help with:
- Loading cargo at pickup
- Securing cargo
- Unloading at delivery
- Handling delivery paperwork (with driver)
- Taking delivery photos (if needed)

### What the Driver Does

The driver handles:
- Starting the trip
- Recording fuel
- Recording most expenses
- Completing the trip

**You assist** - the driver **manages** the app.

### If You Need to Record Expenses

Sometimes you may need to record an expense:

1. Open the active trip
2. Tap "Add Expense"
3. Select expense type (usually Parking or Other)
4. Enter amount
5. Add description
6. Take photo of receipt
7. Tap "Save"

**Ask your driver** before recording expenses.

---

## At Delivery

### Helping with Proof of Delivery

The driver completes the delivery proof, but you can help:

**If Driver Asks You to Take Photos**:
1. Driver gives you the phone
2. Open active trip
3. Tap "Complete Delivery"
4. Driver enters recipient name
5. Driver gets signature
6. You take delivery photos:
   - Tap camera icon
   - Take clear photos of cargo
   - Multiple angles
   - Show condition
7. Driver saves POD

### Delivery Tips

✅ **Good Photos**:
- Clear and well-lit
- Show cargo condition
- Take from different angles
- Include any damage (important!)

✅ **Be Professional**:
- Help unload carefully
- Treat cargo with care
- Be polite to customers
- Report any issues to driver

## Your Payroll

### Viewing Your Pay

Navigate to **"Payroll"** section:
- See current period earnings
- View past pay periods
- Check payment history

### What You'll See

Each payroll shows:
- **Period dates**: e.g., Aug 1-15, 2024
- **Trips worked**: Number of trips you helped with
- **Days worked**: Days you were active
- **Hours worked**: Total hours (if tracked)
- **Gross pay**: Total before deductions
- **Deductions**: Cash advances, NHIF, NSSF, etc.
- **Net pay**: Amount you receive

### Cash Advances

**View Your Advances**:
- Navigate to "Cash Advances"
- See advance details
- Check repayment status

**Repayment**:
- Automatically deducted from payroll
- Balance updates each pay period
- You can see remaining balance

## Troubleshooting

### Can't See My Trips

**Solutions**:
- Pull down to refresh
- Check you're assigned to trips (ask operator)
- Restart app
- Check internet connection

### Photos Won't Upload

**Solutions**:
- Check internet connection
- Wait - photos upload automatically
- Don't close app while uploading
- Contact driver if urgent

### App Crashes

**Solutions**:
- Update app from store
- Restart phone

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I start a trip?**  
A: No, only drivers can start and complete trips.

**Q: Can I record fuel?**  
A: No, only drivers record fuel.

**Q: What if the driver is busy?**  
A: You can help take delivery photos and assist with POD, but driver must approve and save.

**Q: When do I get paid?**  
A: Check with your operator for payroll schedule. You can view your pay in the Payroll section.

**Q: What if I can't work a trip?**  
A: Contact your operator immediately so they can find a replacement.

**Q: Can I see all trips?**  
A: No, you only see trips where you're assigned as porter.
